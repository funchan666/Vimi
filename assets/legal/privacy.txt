Vimi Privacy Policy
Updated November 23, 2026

Vimi is a free Android dance community for adults aged 18 and over. This policy covers the app and this website. For privacy requests or questions, contact Vimi at funchan7777777@gmail.com.

Accounts and public profiles

Vimi processes your email, account ID, display name and date of birth to create and secure an account and apply the 18-and-over rule. Supabase Auth handles password authentication. Your date of birth stays private and is used to check your age eligibility. Sign-in sessions are stored in encrypted device storage.

Your saved display name, dance styles, bio and avatar are visible to signed-in members. Approved dance photos, videos, captions and comments are community content. Likes and follows enable social features. Saved posts, blocks, reports and unpublished posts are not public community lists. Do not add personal information to a public post or profile unless you want other members to see it.

Messages, media and safety

Direct messages and participant identifiers are stored for conversations between mutual follows. Messages are protected in transit. Participants can read the messages and authorized staff can access them when needed for support or moderation. Reports, review decisions and account restrictions help us respond to abuse, copyright concerns and child-safety incidents.

Only media you choose for an avatar or social post is uploaded when you save or submit it. A submitted upload can remain as a private draft if publication is interrupted, until you discard it or delete the account. An external system camera or photo picker handles profile and post capture and selection. Vimi does not request broad photo-library access, precise location, an address book, or access to unrelated files. Selected videos can contain audio.

Voice and video calls

Vimi uses your microphone when you start or answer a call, and uses your camera for video calls. You can mute your microphone, turn your camera off, or end the call. Live audio and video are sent to the other participant over an encrypted WebRTC connection. Vimi does not record or store the call audio or video. Participants may independently record what they see or hear.

Supabase processes participant account IDs, call type, status, timestamps and temporary WebRTC connection details such as IP addresses. This information is used to connect calls, enforce account safety rules and avoid overlapping calls. Connection details in the call database are cleared after calls end or expire. Call metadata is scheduled for deletion about one day after a call ends. Provider logs and backups follow the schedules described below.

Calls use Google’s STUN service by default to check how the devices can reach each other. The service can see network information such as IP addresses. If a relay is configured, it helps carry encrypted call traffic between participants.

Local practice and drafts

Private practice notes, rehearsal-video copies, drill progress and unsent drafts remain on the device in the account where you made them. They are not automatically uploaded or synced to other devices. Sharing a practice video as a social post is a separate action. Local practice drafts and recovery copies use device storage; they are not represented as encrypted cloud backups.

Publisher-provided reference videos and profile-library images load from public Supabase Storage URLs. Anyone with a library media URL can retrieve that publisher content. Member uploads use private storage. Requests for those public library assets, including artwork on this website, expose network information such as an IP address to the provider for delivery and security. Saved library bookmarks and practice-note references stay on the current device and account. A library image becomes part of your account media when you choose it and save your profile.

Website

You can read this website without signing in. The site uses no advertising trackers or analytics scripts. The website host and the media provider can receive standard network and request information to serve pages and assets securely. A support or deletion link opens your email app so you can choose what to send.

Providers and security

Supabase hosts Vimi authentication, database, private uploaded media and Realtime messaging. The Supabase project is hosted in the United States. Providers may process data in other jurisdictions where they operate. Google distributes the app through Google Play and hosts Vimi’s support mailbox. We use the support messages you send to handle your request. Provider request logs can include an IP address, approximate city and country derived from it, request times, browser or app information, session information and technical connection details. Database requests can include search terms and filters in the request URL. Vimi does not request precise location permission or use advertising or separate behavioral analytics SDKs. Vimi does not sell personal data.

Cloud communication uses HTTPS or secure Realtime connections. Account access rules restrict private data and media; authorized administrators can process it for the purposes above. Temporary media links expire, but an already issued link may remain usable until expiry. No security system can prevent every misuse, and members may independently copy content they can view.

Retention and deletion

Profile and social data remain while an account is used, until you remove the content, request deletion or moderation requires action. You can edit your profile, delete posts and comments, block users and delete your account in Settings. You may also request account or specific-data deletion without reinstalling the app by emailing funchan7777777@gmail.com with the subject Vimi account deletion request. Support verifies ownership before acting; never send your password.

Deletion removes the account and its profile, posts, comments, messages, relationships, call records and uploaded account media. The app clears the deleted account’s local practice data on the device completing deletion. To clear copies on another device, remove those copies or clear the app data there. Independently saved copies are outside Vimi’s remote control. Use the account deletion flow or email us to remove the cloud account.

Limited safety and security records may remain for abuse prevention, investigations or legal obligations. These records have restricted access. Provider backups and operational logs expire under their applicable retention schedules. Contact support about retention for a specific request.

Children and updates

Vimi is not intended for people under 18. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited. Use the in-app reporting tools or contact support if you believe a child is at risk or has supplied personal information. See Vimi's Child Safety Standards and Community Guidelines for reporting and enforcement. Material changes to data handling must be reflected in this policy and the app's disclosures.
