Vimi Privacy Policy
Effective November 18, 2026; updated November 19, 2026

Vimi is an adult dance community. For privacy requests or questions, contact funchan7777777@gmail.com. This policy applies to the Vimi Android application and its account, social, practice and coin features.

Accounts and public profiles

Vimi processes your email, password authentication, account ID, display name and date of birth to create and secure an account and apply the 18-and-over rule. Supabase Auth handles password authentication; Vimi does not display passwords to members. The date of birth is private and is not identity verification. Session and pending purchase credentials are stored in encrypted device storage.

Your saved display name, dance styles, bio and avatar are visible to signed-in members. Approved dance photos, videos, captions and comments are community content. Likes and follows enable social features. Saved posts, blocks, reports and unpublished posts are not public community lists. Do not add personal information to a public post or profile unless you want other members to see it.

Messages, media and safety

Direct messages and participant identifiers are stored for conversations between mutual follows. They are not end-to-end encrypted. Participants and authorized support or moderation processing may access them. Reports, review decisions and account restrictions are processed to respond to abuse, copyright concerns and child-safety incidents.

Only media you choose for an avatar or social post is uploaded when you save or submit it. A submitted upload can remain as a private draft if publication is interrupted, until you discard it or delete the account. An external system camera or photo picker handles capture and selection; Vimi does not request broad photo-library access, precise location, an address book, or access to unrelated files. Selected videos can contain audio.

Local practice and drafts

Private practice notes, rehearsal-video copies, drill progress and unsent drafts remain on the device in the account where you made them. They are not automatically uploaded or synced to other devices. Sharing a practice video as a social post is a separate action. Local practice drafts and recovery copies use device storage; they are not represented as encrypted cloud backups.

The reference-video and profile-image libraries are included with the app. Saved library selections and practice notes referring to a library clip stay on the current device/account. Choosing a library profile image does not upload it until you save the profile; that saved image then follows the same public-profile and account-media rules as a photo you select yourself.

Purchases

When purchasing is available, Google Play processes payment. Vimi processes product IDs, purchase tokens, order identifiers, an obfuscated account identifier, coin balances, unlocks and ledger entries for verification, fulfillment, refunds and fraud prevention. Vimi does not receive payment-card numbers. Coins have no cash-out or member-to-member transfer. If purchases are unavailable, the app says so and does not provide an alternative payment method.

Providers and security

Supabase hosts Vimi authentication, database, private uploaded media and Realtime messaging. The current project is hosted in the United States; service processing may occur in other jurisdictions used by the providers. Google processes Play purchases and related store services and hosts Vimi's support mailbox. Support messages you choose to send are processed to handle your request. Provider request logs include IP-derived approximate city and country, request times, user-agent and session information, and TLS/client fingerprints for operation, security and abuse prevention. Search terms and filter parameters sent to the database can also appear in request URLs retained in these logs. Vimi does not request GPS or precise-device-location permission. Vimi does not use an advertising SDK, sell user data or use a separate behavioral analytics SDK in this version.

Cloud communication uses HTTPS or secure Realtime connections. Account access rules restrict private data and media; authorized administrators can process it for the purposes above. Temporary media links expire, but an already issued link may remain usable until expiry. No security system can prevent every misuse, and members may independently copy content they can view.

Retention and deletion

Profile and social data remain while an account is used, until you remove the content, request deletion or moderation requires action. You can edit your profile, delete posts and comments, block users and delete your account in Settings. You may also request account or specific-data deletion without reinstalling the app by emailing funchan7777777@gmail.com with the subject Vimi account deletion request. Support verifies ownership before acting; never send your password.

Deletion removes the account and its profile, posts, comments, messages, relationships and uploaded account media. The app clears the deleted account's local practice data on the device completing deletion. Uninstalling removes local data but does not delete the cloud account. Other device copies and independently saved member copies are outside Vimi's remote control.

Limited transaction and safety audit records may remain for refunds, fraud prevention, security investigations or legal obligations, with direct profile links removed where possible. Remaining order identifiers and restricted safety metadata are not described as fully anonymous. Provider backups and operational logs follow their applicable retention schedules. Retention must remain limited to the stated purpose and applicable obligations; ask support about your specific request.

Children and updates

Vimi is not intended for people under 18. Child sexual abuse and exploitation are prohibited. Use the in-app reporting tools or contact support if you believe a child is at risk or has supplied personal information. See Vimi's Child Safety Standards and Community Guidelines for reporting and enforcement. Material changes to data handling must be reflected in this policy and the app's disclosures.
