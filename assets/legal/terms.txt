Updated November 23, 2026

Vimi is a free dance community for adults aged 18 and over. Accept these Terms of Use and the Community Guidelines before creating an account. Use accurate account information, protect your login and follow the Child Safety Standards.

Sharing and using content

You keep ownership of the content you submit. By sharing it, you give Vimi permission to store, review and display it as needed to operate the service. Only upload work you have permission to share, including the performance, music and any identifiable people.

Practice prompts are general creative guidance, not medical or professional training advice. Choose a safe space and movements suitable for your ability.

Vimi’s publisher video and photo libraries are available for viewing, private rehearsal and the profile choices offered by the app. Get the relevant rights holders’ permission before republishing a performance, image or soundtrack. Studio credits describe editorial clips and imagery. Registered dancer accounts have separate member profiles.

Community conduct

Posts and public comments go through review. Vimi may remove content or restrict accounts that violate these terms. Circumventing blocks, manipulating engagement, sharing illegal material and impersonating a dancer or artist are prohibited. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, sexual solicitation and trafficking of minors are prohibited in all forms, including generated and altered content.

Your account
