Vimi Child Safety Standards
Effective November 19, 2026

Vimi is a dance community for adults aged 18 and over. An age declaration is required to create an account; it is not identity verification. People under 18 must not register or use community messaging.

Vimi prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualization of minors, grooming, sexual solicitation, sextortion, trafficking, and attempts to obtain sexual images or contact from a child. This prohibition includes generated, altered, illustrated and real imagery. A child's apparent consent never makes exploitation acceptable. Do not share, request, promote, link to or sell such material or conduct.

Response and enforcement

Authorized reviewers assess reported content and accounts. Confirmed prohibited material must be removed, and offending accounts may be suspended. A report is not an automatic finding of wrongdoing. Reviewers can remove posts and media, remove comments or messages, and clear violating public profile material. Reporting, blocking and safety controls are free.

When Vimi obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or a child-exploitation offense, the operator must take the action required by applicable law, including reporting confirmed CSAM to the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) or the relevant regional authority where required. Only legally necessary evidence may be preserved with restricted access. Public sharing, ordinary support email and personal devices must not be used to circulate illegal material.

These standards apply to all Vimi users and all user content, including profiles and direct messages. Vimi does not offer a way to disable these protections, and virtual coins cannot buy an exemption.
