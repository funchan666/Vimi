Keep the floor welcoming.

Vimi is for adults aged 18 and over. Respect dancers of every gender, body type, ability and background. Constructive feedback is about movement, timing and practice, never someone's worth or appearance.

Upload only media and music you have permission to share. Credit choreography when appropriate; attribution alone does not give permission. Do not upload someone else's performance, copyrighted songs, personal information or private messages without permission.

No sexual content, exploitation, threats, hate, bullying, scams, impersonation or spam. Never contact someone who has asked you to stop.

New posts and comments are reviewed before appearing to others. Reports enter a moderation queue and hide the reported item for you. Blocking prevents interaction and removes that person's content and conversation from your view. Moderators may remove content or restrict accounts. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Follow and chat thoughtfully. Direct messages require mutual follows. Vimi does not automatically follow accounts or generate messages for other people.
