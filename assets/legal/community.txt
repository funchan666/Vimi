Vimi Community Guidelines
Updated November 19, 2026

Keep the floor welcoming.

Vimi is for adults aged 18 and over. Respect dancers of every gender, body type, ability and background. Constructive feedback is about movement, timing and practice, never someone's worth or appearance.

Upload only media and music you have permission to share. Credit choreography when appropriate; attribution alone does not give permission. Do not upload someone else's performance, copyrighted songs, personal information or private messages without permission. Do not impersonate an artist, label or another member.

No sexual content, exploitation, threats, hate, bullying, scams or spam. Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, sexual solicitation and trafficking of minors are prohibited in every form, including generated or altered content. Never contact someone who has asked you to stop.

New posts and comments enter review before appearing to others. Public profiles and direct messages are also subject to these rules and can be reported. On a post, comment, message or profile, use Report and choose the reason, including Child safety or Underage user. Reports enter a private review queue and hide the reported item for you. Blocking prevents further messaging and removes the relationship and conversation from your view.

Moderators can reject or permanently remove content, clear violating profile material and suspend accounts. They must act on confirmed violations; completing a report is separate from removing its target. Coins do not buy immunity from these standards, and reporting and blocking are always free.

For a child-safety concern you cannot report from an item, contact funchan7777777@gmail.com with identifiers and a description. Do not attach or forward suspected illegal imagery. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. Confirmed CSAM must be removed and reported to NCMEC or the relevant regional authority when required by law. Read Vimi's Child Safety Standards for more information.

Follow and chat thoughtfully. Direct messages require mutual follows. Vimi does not automatically follow accounts or generate messages for other people.
