Vimi Account Deletion
Updated November 23, 2026

Delete in the app

Sign in and open Me, Settings, then Delete account. Enter your current password, select Delete account permanently, and confirm Delete my account. If your account is restricted, choose Delete account from the account-unavailable screen.

Deletion removes the Vimi profile, account credentials, dance posts, comments, messages, follows, blocks, call records and uploaded account media. The app also clears practice notes, drafts and personal rehearsal files stored for that account on the device where deletion is completed.

Request deletion without installing Vimi

Email funchan7777777@gmail.com with the subject Vimi account deletion request. Include the email address used for your Vimi account and, if available, your display name or account ID. Vimi support will verify ownership before deleting the account. Never send your password, a login code or illegal content by email.

This email route is also available for privacy questions and requests to remove specific personal data. Tell support which data or content the request concerns. Uninstalling the app alone removes its local device data but does not delete a cloud account.

What may remain

Limited safety and security records may be retained for abuse prevention, investigations or legal obligations. Access to these records is restricted. Provider backups and operational logs expire under their applicable retention schedules. Contact support about the handling and retention applicable to your request.

Private practice copies on another device cannot be remotely erased by Vimi. Remove those copies or clear Vimi's app data on that device. Deleting a public post does not erase copies another member has already independently saved.
