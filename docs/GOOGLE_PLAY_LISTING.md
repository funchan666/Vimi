### Full description

```text
A chorus is more fun with a crew. Vimi is a dance community for adults who want to share covers, find other dancers, and work on their next routine.

Share your take
Swipe through short dance videos, explore K-pop, girl-group and boy-group covers, and follow dancers whose routines you want to see again. Share your own photo or video, try an eight-count challenge, and save covers for later. Posts and public comments are reviewed before they appear to other members.

Make time for practice
Browse solo, duet, and group reference clips in Studio. Adjust playback speed, mirror the video, and loop the clip as you practice. Build a routine with practice plans and reusable checklists. Keep notes on tempo and steps, set a weekly goal, and track the minutes you log. Your private practice journal stays on your device.

Find your dance people
Add the styles you dance to your profile and discover new dancers. When you follow each other, you can message, send a rehearsal invitation, or start a one-to-one voice or video call.

Vimi is free to use and for adults 18 and over. You can report content and block accounts in the app. Account deletion is available in Settings.
```
